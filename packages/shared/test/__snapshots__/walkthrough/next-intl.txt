## LoginForm submit  /[locale]/login  3 requests
1. Submit the form in `LoginForm` on `/[locale]/login`
2. It sends `POST /auth/login`
3. It goes to `/[locale]/verify`
4. Submit the form in `VerifyOtpForm` on `/[locale]/verify`
5. It sends `POST /auth/verify-otp`
6. It goes to `/[locale]/dashboard`
7. The page loads `GET /me`
